### Abstract

Structural and electronic properties of Sc-decorated black phosphorus (SP) and its gas sensing for NO molecule were investigated using density functional theory with the emphasis on the response to applied strain.

It was found that SP transformed into an indirect bandgap semiconductor and converted into a metallic characteristic under strain. SP showed strong interaction with NO with a high adsorption energy of $-2.92\text{ eV}$ due to orbital hybridization and extensive charge transfer, exhibiting a 3.3-fold sensitivity compared to pristine black phosphorus, suggesting that SP is a potential NO sensor. Furthermore, strain engineering provided an effective route to facilitate the desorption of NO.
