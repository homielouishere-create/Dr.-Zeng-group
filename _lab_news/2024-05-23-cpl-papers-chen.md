2021-level Materials Physics undergraduate students **Houru Chen** and **Zilin Chen** from the School of Materials and New Energy, South China Normal University (SCNU), have published research articles as first authors in the international SCI journal ***Chemical Physics Letters*** (JCR Q2, IF = 2.8).

## 1. Strain Engineering & Gas-Sensing Properties of Sc-Decorated Black Phosphorus

Undergraduate **Houru Chen** and **Longying Wen** co-authored the paper titled *"Theoretical insights into the structural, electronic and gas-sensing properties of Sc-decorated black phosphorus via strain engineering"*.

- **Key Findings**: Using first-principles DFT calculations, the team investigated the adsorption and sensing behavior of Sc-decorated black phosphorus (SP) towards toxic nitric oxide (NO) gas. Applied strain induces a semiconductor-to-metal transition in SP, enhancing NO adsorption energy to -2.92 eV (a 3.3-fold increase in sensitivity compared to pristine BP). Strain engineering was further demonstrated as an effective knob to facilitate gas desorption.
- **Affiliations**: SCNU is the primary affiliation, with Sichuan Police College as the collaborating institution. **Prof. Huadong Zeng** (SCNU) and **Vice Prof. Hao Jiang** (Sichuan Police College) served as co-corresponding authors.

## 2. Optoelectronic Tuning of Phenyl-Functionalized Black Phosphorus

Undergraduate **Zilin Chen** published as sole first author the paper titled *"Uniaxial strain engineering of the electronic and optical properties of phenyl-functionalized black phosphorus"*.

- **Key Findings**: The study demonstrated that uniaxial strain modulates the band structure and optical absorption of phenyl-functionalized black phosphorus (phenyl-BP). Under 4% tensile strain along the armchair direction, phenyl-BP retains a p-type semiconductor state with a 0.60 eV bandgap, while compressive strain significantly boosts UV absorption around 14 eV, providing theoretical guidance for optoelectronic device design.
- **Affiliations**: SCNU is the sole author affiliation for this work, with **Prof. Huadong Zeng** as the corresponding author.
