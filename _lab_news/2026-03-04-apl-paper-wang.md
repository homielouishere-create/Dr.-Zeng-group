Recently, **Zihao Wang**, a 2023-level undergraduate majoring in Energy Storage Science and Engineering at the School of Materials and New Energy, South China Normal University (SCNU), published a first-author research paper titled *"The cooperative cation-π interaction in two-dimensional THD-C as a high-capacity anode material for Li-ion batteries"* in the Nature Index journal ***Applied Physics Letters*** (JCR Q2, IF = 3.6).

South China Normal University is the first affiliation of the paper, with Sichuan University as the collaborating institution. The co-corresponding authors are **Prof. Huadong Zeng** (School of Materials and New Energy, SCNU) and **Prof. Xinlu Cheng** (Sichuan University).

## Research Highlights

Based on first-principles density functional theory (DFT) calculations, the study systematically evaluates the feasibility of two-dimensional sp–sp² hybridized THD-C carbon allotrope as an anode material for lithium-ion batteries:

- **Core Mechanism**: Uncovers the fundamental interaction mechanism where spatially adjacent acetylenic bonds induce Li-C coupling through cooperative cation-π interactions driven by partial electron transfer.
- **Battery Performance**: THD-C exhibits a high theoretical Li storage capacity of **1116.7 mAh/g**, a low Li diffusion barrier of **0.49 eV**, a moderate open-circuit voltage of **0.40 V**, and retains a minimal 3% volume expansion during cycling.

This work provides critical theoretical support for exploring high-performance carbon-based anode materials in next-generation lithium-ion battery technologies.
